About us

Commercial kitchen cleaning done properly.

HygieneXperts provides specialist cleaning services for commercial kitchens, food service venues and hospitality facilities that need dependable hygiene, grease control and safe, efficient operations.

Built around kitchen performance and compliance.

We specialise in the cleaning and maintenance required in demanding commercial kitchen environments. From extractor systems and ductwork to fryers, cool rooms and food prep areas, our focus is on creating cleaner, safer and more efficient workspaces.

Our approach is practical and thorough. Every job is planned around real operational needs, with careful attention to grease build-up, airflow management, hygiene standards and the day-to-day rhythm of busy food businesses.

HygieneXperts works with hospitality teams, restaurants, cafes, food manufacturers and commercial operators who need reliable service with clear communication, consistent quality and measurable results.
