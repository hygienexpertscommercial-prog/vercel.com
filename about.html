About us

Commercial kitchen cleaning specialists serving food businesses with care and precision.

HygieneXperts exists to help commercial kitchens stay clean, safe and operationally efficient. We focus on specialist cleaning services that support compliance, protect equipment and create a better experience for staff and customers.

Our story

Built around quality, safety and kitchen performance.

We recognised a gap in the market: many businesses needed a partner who understood the strict hygiene demands of commercial kitchens, not just a generic cleaner. That is why HygieneXperts specialises in high-risk cleaning areas such as rangehoods, exhaust systems, fryer equipment, cool rooms and hard-to-access kitchen surfaces.

Our approach is straightforward: clean thoroughly, work safely and help your business maintain a high standard of food safety and presentation. We combine specialist cleaning expertise with a practical understanding of hospitality operations, so we can work efficiently without disrupting service.

Why choose us

Focused on commercial kitchen standards.

Our team works with cafes, restaurants, takeaway businesses, hospitality venues, clubs and food production spaces. We understand the issues that matter most: grease build-up, poor airflow, compliance risk, unsanitary surfaces and equipment wear.

Whether you need routine maintenance or a deep clean, we deliver service with attention to detail, clear communication and dependable scheduling.
