Our services

Commercial kitchen cleaning services for Sydney food businesses.

HygieneXperts delivers specialist solutions for restaurants, cafes, commercial kitchens and high-volume food service spaces that require reliable, compliant and thorough cleaning.

Rangehood, Canopy & Filter Cleaning

Removes built-up grease, odours and residue from extraction systems to improve airflow and reduce fire risk.

Exhaust Fan & Duct Cleaning

Clears venting and ductwork to restore efficiency, improve ventilation and support a safer kitchen environment.

Deep Kitchen & Cool Room Cleaning

Targets grease residue, hidden grime and operational spill zones across kitchen floors, walls and cold storage spaces.

Equipment & Fryer Cleaning

Restores fryers, prep surfaces and commercial kitchen equipment for cleaner operation and better food safety standards.

Cooking Oil Change Service

Assists with oil disposal and system maintenance to keep kitchen equipment operating efficiently and hygienically.

Duct Access Panel Installation

Improves future maintenance access by creating clean, code-conscious access points in kitchen duct systems.

High-Pressure Cleaning

Removes heavy buildup from external kitchen surfaces, hard-to-clean walkways and operational areas requiring deep sanitation.
