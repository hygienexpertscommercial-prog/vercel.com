Commercial kitchen expertise

Specialist services for busy food service environments.

From canopies and filters to exhaust ductwork and fryers, our cleaning service supports safe operations, better airflow and a cleaner presentation for your customers and staff.

01

Rangehood, Canopy & Filter Cleaning

Removes heavy grease buildup from rangehoods, canopies and filters to improve airflow, reduce fire risk and maintain kitchen cleanliness.

02

Exhaust Fan & Duct Cleaning

Deep cleans fan housings and ductwork to improve air quality, protect equipment and support safer kitchen ventilation.

03

Deep Kitchen & Cool Room Cleaning

Targets high-touch and hard-to-reach surfaces in kitchens and cool rooms for a more hygienic and well-maintained operation.

04

Equipment & Fryer Cleaning

Thorough cleaning and degreasing of fryers and equipment to help maintain food safety and improve equipment longevity.

05

Cooking Oil Change Service

Supports fryer performance and food quality with scheduled oil change and related maintenance support.

06

Duct Access Panel Installation

Installs access panels to make future filter and duct cleaning easier, quicker and more efficient for ongoing maintenance.

07

High-Pressure Cleaning

Ideal for external kitchen zones, service areas and hard surfaces where grease and buildup need deeper pressure cleaning.
